# Preregistered simulation protocol

## Primary question

How often does each receiver-control policy recover every expected synthetic
frame under a controlled CubeSat-like baseband channel?

The primary unit is a paired run, not an individual tool call or IQ sample. Run
success requires every frame payload to match the generated bytes after a valid
frame check. The primary contrast is the paired difference in run success.
Frame correctness is secondary and is analyzed with the run as the cluster;
individual frames are not independent mission replications.
The estimand is the marginal paired difference under equal weighting of every
frozen factorial cell. It is not an estimate of the natural frequency of channel
conditions in an operational satellite population.

## Frozen factors

The full profile crosses:

- protocol: AX.25/AFSK or bounded CCSDS-inspired/BPSK;
- peak SNR: 16, 10, 5 or 0 dB;
- normalized Doppler peak: 0, 0.001 or 0.003 cycles/sample;
- constant normalized frequency offset: 0 or 0.004 cycles/sample;
- sample-clock error: 0 or 50 ppm;
- burst erasure: absent or enabled;
- fractional-delay two-ray multipath: absent or enabled;
- carrier phase inversion: absent or 180 degrees.

This gives 768 cells. Thirty paired replications per cell give 23,040 paired
runs. With two primary policies there are 46,080 policy records. Eight independently
generated frames are attempted in each run.

## Randomization and pairing

The preregistered master seed is expanded with `SeedSequence`. Cell identity and
replication index are incorporated through SHA-256-derived spawn words. Channel
and policy streams are separate. Both policies process identical IQ.

## Analysis

Report numerator and denominator for run success and frame correctness. Report
Wilson 95% intervals per policy, exact paired McNemar on discordant runs, and a
paired bootstrap interval for the mean success difference. Stratified tables are
descriptive unless the dissertation preregisters a causal model before running
the full profile.

No hypothesis test is performed separately in all 768 cells. Protocol, SNR,
Doppler, CFO, clock, burst, multipath and phase summaries remain descriptive unless a
multiple-comparison procedure is frozen before the full campaign. Secondary-tap
magnitudes are fixed by cell, while their phases vary by seeded replication.

The bounded search budget is five CFO hypotheses in frozen order: `-0.01`,
`-0.004`, `0`, `0.004`, `0.01`, each tested at zero and 180-degree phase. It
therefore permits at most ten correction hypotheses and stops at the first
frame-valid result. The
optional `--ablations` policy decodes without correction; it diagnoses the
contribution of frequency search and is not a third primary comparator.

Failure classes are frozen by decoder messages: missing framing/synchronization,
invalid FCS/CRC, invalid packet length, unsupported profile, search exhaustion,
or payload mismatch. Counts are events nested in runs and are not independent
replications.

## Negative and positive controls

The `controls` profile crosses both protocols with 30 dB positive and -20 dB
negative controls. Positive controls must decode for the manifest-aware baseline;
negative controls are expected to approach zero. The `stress` profile isolates
CFO, Doppler, clock error, burst loss, multipath, impulses, clipping and a gain
invariance check. These diagnostic campaigns are separate from the primary full
factorial. Protocol round-trips and corruption rejection are unit-tested
independently of the channel.

## Frozen quality gates

The `quality` command first requires a successful regenerative audit and exact
paired-run and policy-record counts. For the separate controls campaign, each
primary policy must recover at least 95% of positive-control runs and no more
than 5% of negative-control runs. For the full campaign, each primary policy
must contain at least one success and one failure. These gates test instrument
behavior and dataset informativeness; they do not require a favorable policy
effect or a significant hypothesis test. They were fixed before the version
0.2.0 confirmatory campaign was observed.
